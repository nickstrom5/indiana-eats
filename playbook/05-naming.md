# Indiana Eats: name, icon and palette (final: Nick's decision 2026-11-07, "Yes, as proposed")

## Name
- **App Store name:** "Indiana Eats: Restaurants" (25). **Subtitle:** "Tenderloins, Pie & Drive-Ins" (28). **Home Screen:** "Indiana
  Eats" (12).
- Checked 2026-11-06 with Apple's public search: no app is named "Indiana Eats", "Hoosier Eats" or "Crossroads Eats" (results are
  IndyStar, delivery apps and IU Hoosiers). "Indiana Eats" mirrors Wisconsin Eats and is the plain search fit.
- Alternatives from the prompt: "Hoosier Eats" (IU's teams are the Hoosiers: check IU's marks first) and "Crossroads Eats" (the state
  motto; "Crossroads" is a common restaurant name). Avoided: Visit Indiana's "IN Indiana" (no "Eat IN Indiana"), "Honest-to-Goodness
  Indiana", "Indiana Grown"; "Indianapolis Dining" and "Indianapolis Tenderloin Week" apps exist.

## Icon
- **A (final, Nick 2026-11-07): a breaded pork tenderloin sandwich, front view, the cutlet far wider than the bun**, on the flag's blue.
  `scripts/icon-concepts.swift`, `scripts/make-brand.swift`.
- B: a slice of sugar cream pie from the side (reads as a wedge). C: an ear of sweet corn (reads as an arrow at 60 px).
- All three pass `state-prompts/icon-style/check.swift`. No team, university or speedway marks.

## Palette (the state flag; IU, Purdue and Notre Dame split the state)
- Flag blue #000F5D (17.6:1 on white): titles, tint, accents.
- Brick red #8E2A1E (white on it 8.6:1): hand-checked guide cards, focus rings. Deep brick #6E1F15: honors chips.
- Flag gold #D59F0F: fills only (pins, the title shadow), under blue text (7.2:1); never text on white (2.4:1).
- Corn-silk cream #F6F1E3: pages.
- Header: a checkered stripe (two rows of blue and white squares) over a gold baseline; no Indy 500 or Speedway marks.
