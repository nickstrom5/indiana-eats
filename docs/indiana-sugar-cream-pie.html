Sugar cream and the rest

Visit Indiana's Best Pies list names sugar cream pie at Clifty Inn in Clifty Falls State Park, peanut butter pie at the Olde Richmond Inn, pecan pie at the Fort Harrison State Park Inn, berry pie at Das Dutchman Essenhaus, cobblers at the Stone Hearth Café and mini peanut butter pies at the Abe Martin Lodge in Brown County State Park. Wick's Pie's own shop in Winchester, also on the list, is a bakery rather than a dining room.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Nashville (1), Madison (1), Middlebury (1), Indianapolis (1), Richmond (1), Centerville (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (a dated story, or an Indiana tourism office's own page for it) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.
