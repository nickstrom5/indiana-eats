Indiana's oldest restaurants

Saloons and drive-ins that have fed Indiana since 1960 or before: 6 places in all, oldest first. The oldest, Knickerbocker Saloon in Lafayette, goes back to 1835.

Which year counts

We count from the year a place opened as it stands today, going by a dated source that ties the year to it. A building's age isn't the business's: The Log Inn in Haubstadt dates its building to 1825, which isn't when it opened as a restaurant, so it isn't dated here.

Oldest first

  1. Knickerbocker Saloon

    113 N 5th St, Lafayette

    Since 1835

    A saloon on North 5th Street in Lafayette since 1835.

    Tasting Table's oldest bar in every state (February 2025): Indiana's is the Knickerbocker Saloon, operating since 1835.

  2. Triple XXX Family Restaurant

    2 N Salisbury St, West Lafayette

    Drive-in Since 1929

    Indiana's first drive-in, opened in 1929, per Visit Indiana; root beer on tap.

    Visit Indiana (June 2026): opened in West Lafayette in 1929 as Indiana's first drive-in.

  3. Kunkel's Drive In

    2402 N Park Rd, Connersville

    Drive-in Since 1954

    Open since 1954: daily specials and homemade pies.

    Visit Indiana (June 2026): open since 1954.

  4. Dog n Suds

    52 South St, Lafayette

    Drive-in Since 1956

    Locally owned since 1956: the Coney Dog, the Boilermaker and root beer.

    Visit Indiana (June 2026): the Lafayette location, locally owned since 1956.

  5. The Suds

    350 Market Plz, Greenwood

    Drive-in Since 1957

    Opened as a Dog n Suds in 1957: burgers, shakes and classic cars.

    Visit Indiana (June 2026): opened as a Dog n Suds in 1957.

  6. Bo-Mac's Drive Inn

    408 4th St, Shoals

    Drive-in Since 1960

    Carhop service since 1960: onion rings, shakes and sundaes.

    Visit Indiana (June 2026): run by the Ragsdale family from 1960.
